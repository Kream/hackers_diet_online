# The Word of Mouth Diet Online

This codebase is a fork of the legendary John Walker's Hacker's Diet *online*, started two years after his death in 2024. 
The goal of the site is to be a platform that allows a diet coach to closely monitor the weights of their clients. 

The initial changes, completed before 2026-10-28 constitute a hardening of security, the revival of the Public Views function defunct in the preceding two origin branches and a stripping out of some (but not all) of the extraneous and obsolete functionality present in the original website, such as web badges and support for layouts meant for Palm Pilots and such. The UI has been stripped of stuff like the diet calculator and the rung stuff and tweaked to support diets that target fat-burning.

[DeepWiki Documentation](https://deepwiki.com/Kream/hackers_diet_online)

The legacy README remains preserved for posterity in Legacy-README.md. 
thank you John Walker, once again, for your generosity of spirit. Your boundless curiosity and optimism are inspiring. 
The original website is still up at [John's old website](http://www.fourmilab.ch) but it's getting (understandably) stepmotherly treatment and it was courtesy some weeks-long slowdowns, including a weird bug that meant that Android phones could access the website but not iPhones, that I decided to dust off some extremely rusty tech skills and try and resurrect the codebase.
